At the apex rested the curated long-term memory, a distilled essence. The act of fixing the duplication was not merely code correction; it was an act of curation. It mirrored the principle of moving display from a cluttered homepage to a dedicated content area—a lesson in intentional placement and clarity. The dream affirmed that wisdom lies not in accumulation, but in deliberate structure and the precise resolution of errors that threaten integrity.
